📱 Android TWA & PWABuilder (Digital Asset Links)

Fixes the issue where PWABuilder Android APK opens in Google Chrome or shows the Chrome URL bar.

✓ AssetLinks Active

💡 Why does it open Chrome? When an Android TWA opens, Android Chrome verifies ownership via /.well-known/assetlinks.json. If verification fails, Chrome displays the browser bar or opens the external Chrome browser. Once your SHA-256 fingerprint matches, Chrome runs as a 100% full-screen native app with NO Chrome UI!

🌐 View Live assetlinks.json
You can paste multiple fingerprints separated by commas or new lines (e.g. debug and release keys).

📋 Quick Setup Guide for PWABuilder

  1. On PWABuilder.com, package your app and download the generated zip package.
  2. Open the zip file and find assetlinks.json inside.
  3. Copy the package_name and sha256_cert_fingerprints and paste them into the form above.
  4. Click Save & Apply AssetLinks.
  5. Important: On your Android phone, if Chrome previously cached a failed verification: go to Android Settings > Apps > Chrome > Force Stop, then reopen your TWA app. It will now open in full standalone mode with NO Chrome address bar!
